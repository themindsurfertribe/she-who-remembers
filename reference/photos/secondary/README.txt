==================================================
CAPTURE GUIDANCE
==================================================

- Clean the lens first.
- Use full native resolution.
- Avoid digital zoom.
- Prefer morning or late-day natural light.
- Avoid cluttered backgrounds.
- Leave generous crop room. Do not pin the subject to the edge.
- Shoot landscape and portrait when the scene supports both.
- Take several framings before moving the setup.
- Avoid Portrait Mode if edge separation looks artificial.
- Avoid excessive HDR or filters.
- Do not bake text into the photograph.
- Retain original files.

Brand test:
Does this reveal the world of She Who Remembers, or merely decorate the page?

Visual ratio to preserve:
70% clean editorial elegance
20% cosmic or sacred atmosphere
10% dramatic moments

==================================================
INTAKE REVIEW (NEXT STEP, AFTER FILES ARRIVE)
==================================================

Do not publish during intake review.

For every file:

1. Inventory the file
2. Record filename
3. Record dimensions
4. Record orientation
5. Identify duplicates or near-duplicates
6. Assess technical quality
7. Assess crop flexibility
8. Assess brand fit
9. Assign potential page placement
10. Classify as one of:
    strong production candidate
    alternate candidate
    social-only candidate
    reference-only
    reject

Only photographs that pass a later explicit approval step may be copied to production.
